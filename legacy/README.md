# Previous homepage design

The original homepage and its stylesheet are preserved here unchanged. Copies of
the citations and terms pages are also included with their previous styling.

To restore the previous design from the repository root:

```sh
cp legacy/index.html index.html
cp legacy/index.css static/css/index.css
cp legacy/citations.html citations.html
cp legacy/terms-of-use.html terms-of-use.html
```

The citations and terms copies retain their content and old stylesheet reference.
